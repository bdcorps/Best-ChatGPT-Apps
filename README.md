# Best-ChatGPT-Apps

Hey there! 👋 Welcome to **Best-ChatGPT-Apps** - basically a handpicked collection of the coolest ChatGPT apps out there. Think of it as your go-to spot for discovering awesome stuff that people are building with ChatGPT.

You know how there are tons of ChatGPT apps popping up everywhere? Well, we've been scouring the internet to find the best ones and put them all in one place. Whether you're a developer looking for some inspiration, someone who just wants to find a cool app to use, or maybe you've built something yourself that you want to share - you're in the right place!

The whole idea here is pretty simple: we want to celebrate all the amazing work people are doing with ChatGPT. There's so much creativity happening in this space, and honestly, it's pretty wild to see what folks are coming up with. From productivity tools to creative assistants, educational stuff, entertainment - you name it, people are building it.

Oh, and quick shoutout - this whole thing was inspired by the awesome [awesome-gpt-store](https://github.com/f/awesome-gpt-store) repo. We loved that concept and thought, "Hey, what if we did something similar but for full apps instead of just custom gpts?" So here we are!

## Add a ChatGPT App

So you've got a ChatGPT app you want to share? Or maybe you stumbled across something really cool that deserves more attention? Perfect! That's exactly what this repo is for.

We're all about giving great apps the spotlight they deserve. There's so much cool stuff being built out there, and honestly, some of it doesn't get the recognition it should. That's where you come in - help us find the gems!

Here's the deal:

- 🎙️ **Want to add your app?** Just follow this format: `* [GPT's name](http://example.com/) - A short description.` Keep it simple, tell us what makes it cool, and you're good to go!

- ✅ **What's in it for you?** Well, your app could end up being featured here and getting seen by tons of people who are actually interested in this stuff. Plus, you'll be part of a community that's genuinely excited about what you're building. Pretty neat, right?

We're always looking for apps that do something interesting - whether that's helping people be more productive, sparking creativity, teaching something new, making people laugh, or solving a real problem. If it's built with ChatGPT and it's cool, we want to know about it!

# Contributing

Here's how to [get involved](https://github.com/bdcorps/Best-ChatGPT-Apps/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) first. Don't worry, they're not super complicated or anything. There are lots of ways you can help out:
- Found a cool ChatGPT app? Add it!
- Built something yourself? Share it!
- See an entry that could use a better description? Fix it!
- Notice something broken or outdated? Let us know!
- Got ideas for how we could make this better? We're all ears!

Got questions? Confused about something? Just want to chat? Hit me up [@thisissukh_](https://twitter.com/thisissukh_) on Twitter or open an issue here on GitHub. I'm pretty responsive, and I'm always down to talk about ChatGPT apps or help you figure things out. Don't be shy!
